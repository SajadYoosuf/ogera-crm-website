Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026  ·  Ogera Ventures Private Limited

Who We Are

Ogera CRM is a product of Ogera Ventures Private Limited, registered in Malappuram, Kerala, India. This policy explains what personal and business data we collect when you use our platform, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

By using Ogera CRM, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data We Collect

  • Business and contact details you enter into the CRM — leads, customers, follow-up notes, quotations, and pipeline records
  • Your name, phone number, and email address when you sign up or book a demo
  • Usage logs (login times, feature activity) to monitor platform performance and security
  • Device type and browser information for troubleshooting and compatibility
  • WhatsApp message records you choose to sync through the CRM's WhatsApp integration

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Ogera CRM platform for your business
  • To respond to demo requests, onboarding queries, and support tickets
  • To send important service and security updates — you can opt out of marketing messages at any time
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorised access to your account
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations in India

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

WhatsApp and Meta Integration

If you connect Ogera CRM to your WhatsApp Business account, messages are processed through the official Meta Business API under Meta's own terms of service. We store only the conversation records you explicitly choose to sync into the CRM. We do not read, store, or access any messages outside the sync scope you configure. We do not use your WhatsApp data for advertising or share it with any third party.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured servers located within India. We use industry-standard protections including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and regular security reviews. Only authorised team members can access production data, and only when necessary for support or legal compliance.

Data Retention

We keep your business data for as long as your account remains active. If you close your account, we permanently delete your data within 30 days, unless a legal obligation requires us to retain it for a longer period.

Your Rights

  • Access or download your data at any time from account settings
  • Request correction of any inaccurate records we hold
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent and opt out of marketing communications
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 3 working days.

Cookies

Ogera CRM uses only essential session cookies required to keep you signed in. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email or by an in-app notice at least 7 days before the change takes effect. Continued use of the platform after that date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
